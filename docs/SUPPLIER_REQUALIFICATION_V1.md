# Supplier Regulatory Qualification & Requalification v1

## Purpose
Control supplier regulatory qualification and requalification where supplier evidence supports ingredient and product regulatory decisions.

## Qualification chain
Supplier → Supplier Regulatory Evidence → Ingredient/RM → Compliance Profile → Formula → Product Regulatory Decision.

## Evidence
Required document types and received evidence are explicitly recorded. Evidence should remain traceable to its supplier and applicable ingredient(s).

## Requalification triggers
Requalification may be triggered by document expiry, regulatory change, ingredient change, supplier change, nonconformity, audit finding, or periodic review.

## Status control
Qualification status supports DRAFT, UNDER_REVIEW, QUALIFIED, CONDITIONAL, SUSPENDED, DISQUALIFIED, and EXPIRED. A supplier must not be treated as qualified merely because a record exists; required evidence and review status must be controlled.

## Boundary
This module does not declare a supplier or ingredient legally compliant by itself. Regulatory conclusions remain dependent on applicable current requirements and supporting evidence.
