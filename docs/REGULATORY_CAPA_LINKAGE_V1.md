# Regulatory Action & CAPA Linkage v1

## Purpose
Link regulatory findings to controlled CAPA actions and retain traceability from the finding through effectiveness verification and closure.

## Finding sources
Supported finding sources include Noncompliance, Regulatory Gap, Authority Query, Audit Finding, Complaint, and Incident.

## CAPA linkage
A regulatory finding may require containment, corrective action, preventive action, and an effectiveness check. The finding retains its CAPA reference and supporting evidence.

## Closure control
Where an effectiveness check is required, closure requires supporting evidence. Historical findings and CAPA relationships remain traceable and are not silently overwritten.

## Traceability
Regulatory Source/Query/Audit → Finding → CAPA → Root Cause → Containment/Correction → Corrective/Preventive Action → Effectiveness Check → Evidence → Closure.

## Boundary
This module controls workflow linkage. It does not determine legal severity or make a regulatory conclusion without the applicable source and evidence.
