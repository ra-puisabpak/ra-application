# Supplier & Ingredient Compliance Evidence v1

## Traceability
Supplier → Ingredient/RM → Supplier Evidence → Ingredient Compliance Profile → Formula Ingredient → Regulatory Decision.

## Supplier evidence
Supported evidence types include specification, COA, allergen declaration, GMO declaration, processing-aid declaration, additive declaration and regulatory certificate.

## Ingredient compliance profile
The profile can record allergen status, additive INS, functional class, maximum permitted level, actual use level, processing-aid status, carry-over relevance, linked evidence and the date on which the regulatory source was checked.

## Approval-use rule
An ingredient compliance profile must be VERIFIED, have a checked-source date, and have linked VERIFIED evidence before it can be used as regulatory approval evidence.

## Regulatory boundary
INS number alone is not treated as proof of legal permission. Product category, applicable regulation, functional class, maximum permitted level, actual use, carry-over, restrictions and effective source date remain separate controlled inputs for regulatory review.
