# CAPA & Nonconformity Integration v1

## Purpose
Connect audit, regulatory, supplier, complaint, label, process, product and data-integrity findings to a controlled NC/CAPA lifecycle.

## Flow
Source Finding → Nonconformity → Root Cause Analysis → Containment/Correction → Corrective/Preventive Action → Implementation → Effectiveness Check → Closure.

## Traceability
A Nonconformity may reference its source record. CAPA actions may reference Evidence IDs. Closure retains owner, root cause, effectiveness criteria, verifier and verification date.

## Closure gate
A CAPA cannot close unless root cause and effectiveness criteria are recorded, all actions are completed, and effectiveness verification is documented.

The contract does not prescribe a specific root-cause conclusion; the actual evidence and investigation determine the cause.
