# Change Control Impact Engine v1

## Purpose
Provide a common impact-assessment contract before a post-approval or controlled change is implemented.

## Trigger examples
Product Name, Brand, Formula, Ingredient, Supplier, Additive, Process, Equipment, Site, Packaging, Label, Net Weight, Shelf Life and Storage.

## Impact areas
Registration, Label, Formula, Specification, HACCP, Shelf Life, Allergen, Customer Approval, SOP, WI, FM, Training, Supplier, Process Flow and Validation.

## Control rule
Each identified impact must have a decision and, where applicable, linked required records. A change control with unresolved mandatory impacts must not be treated as IMPLEMENTED.

## Boundary
The engine identifies review areas from the trigger; it does not determine the final regulatory/legal conclusion. RA/QA and the applicable controlled evidence determine the actual decision.
