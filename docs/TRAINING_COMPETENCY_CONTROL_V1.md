# Training & Competency Control v1

## Purpose
Control training and competency evidence arising from controlled document revisions, regulatory changes, process changes, role competency requirements, and CAPA.

## Training triggers
A training requirement can be linked to a specific source record and document revision. Applicable role IDs, due date, status, and completion evidence are retained.

## Competency
Competency records retain the user, competency code, assessment date, assessor, validity period, evidence, and status.

## Effective implementation
Where training is mandatory, the DCC Effective Gate must not allow implementation until the required training is completed and supported by evidence.

## Audit trail
Training assignment, completion, waiver, competency assessment, expiry, and reassessment should remain historically traceable. Records must not be silently overwritten.

## Boundary
This contract defines control and evidence requirements. It does not determine which roles require training unless that requirement is configured by the responsible process owner.
